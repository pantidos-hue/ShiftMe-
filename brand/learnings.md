# Brand learnings

## 2026-08-23 — Copy preference
- **Chose Contrarian over Control** for the working-mom burnout campaign.
- Context: landing page for the anti-vision process, problem-aware audience.
- Angle that won: attacking the premise of the mainstream advice ("stop trying to
  figure out what you want") rather than leading with pain math and process clarity.
- Implication for future copy: lead with the reframe, not the benefit. This brand's
  edge is that the standard approach is *wrong for this reader*, not that this
  version is *better*. Open by removing blame for a failure she's been carrying,
  then hand her the easier question.
- Keep: the frequency-vs-intensity line and the "personality problem vs scheduling
  problem" reframe. Both tested well in review as the sharpest ideas in the piece.
