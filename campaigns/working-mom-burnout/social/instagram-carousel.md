# Carousel — 8 slides

1. Ask me what I want: silence. Ask me what drained me Tuesday: twenty answers in a row.
2. That's not a clarity problem. That's data you've filed under "complaining."
3. Step 1 — Ten minutes. Everything from the last month that made you tense, resentful, or bored. Don't be fair to anyone.
4. Step 2 — Pick one. Ask "why does that bother me" three times. The third answer is the real one.
5. "I dread bedtime" → the only hour that's mine starts when I've got nothing left.
6. Thirty complaints collapse into about six of those. Six is a list you can do something with.
7. Rank by how OFTEN, not how much. The daily 20-minute thing beats the twice-a-year disaster. Every time.
8. Top three. That's your month. Everything else waits — and most of it will have fixed itself.

**Caption:**
The reason "picture your ideal life" never works on you: you're being asked to invent something out of nothing at 9pm on four hours of sleep.

Go the other direction. You already have the evidence.
