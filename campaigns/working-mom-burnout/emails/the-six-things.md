# Body

Ask a burned-out woman what she wants and she goes quiet.

Ask her what drained her last Tuesday and she'll give you twenty things before her tea goes cold.

Same brain. Same hour. Completely different result.

That gap is the whole reason the usual advice bounces off you. It keeps asking the question you can't answer and skipping the one you can.

So flip it. Ten minutes, timer on, write down everything from the last month that made you tense, resentful, or bored. Don't edit. Don't be fair to anyone.

Then take one item and ask why it bothers you. Three times.

"I dread bedtime." Why? Because it's the end of the day. Why does that bother me? Because the hour that's supposed to be mine starts when I'm empty. Why does *that* bother me? Because I only get myself when there's nothing left of me.

That's not a bedtime problem. That's a *when* problem. And a when-problem has a fix, which "I'm exhausted" never will.

Thirty items collapse into six of those. Six is a list you can act on.

Tomorrow I'll send you how to rank them — and why the thing that annoys you most is almost never the one to change first.
